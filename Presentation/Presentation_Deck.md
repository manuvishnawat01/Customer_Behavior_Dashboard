## 🚚 Slide 6: Operational SLA & Logistics Bottlenecks

- **Cancellation Analysis:** 20.3% cancellation rate tied heavily to rainy weather spikes and delivery delays exceeding 40 mins.

## 🚀 Slide 9: Strategic Recommendations for Executive Management
1. **Logistics SLA Optimization:** Deploy rain-surge fleet incentives during adverse weather to cap delivery times below 40 mins and reduce cancellations by 15%.
2. **Loyalty Program Expansion:** Convert High-Value Returning Customers to Paid Membership with targeted perks (Free Delivery & priority dispatch).
3. **Cuisine & Restaurant Partnerships:** Expand Pizza & Biryani merchant partnerships in high-performing markets like Lucknow, Jaipur, and Greater Noida.
4. **Payment Channel Optimization:** Offer cash-back incentives on UPI & Credit Card to reduce Cash-on-Delivery operational overhead.

## ❓ Slide 10: Final Defense Q&A Preparation
- **Q1: Why is the cancellation rate 20.3%?**  
  *Answer:* Analysis reveals cancellations peak during extreme weather (Rainy/Cloudy) and when estimated delivery times exceed 45 minutes.
- **Q2: What is the ROI on discounts?**  
  *Answer:* Moderate discounts (1-20%) drive 65%+ of net revenue without diluting profit margins, whereas 30%+ discounts show diminishing returns.
